Where the hybrid's gain comes from (study P106)

An AVERAGE over all 792 test days, not this day.

P106 refitted the hybrid without one group of inputs at a time and measured how much of the gain was lost.

The five separate groups' shares add up to 49% at 15 minutes; the rest of the gain is shared, because when one group is removed others can partly stand in for it.

A hybrid that sees only what mcsGARCH sees (past one-minute returns, the time of day and the expiry-day flag) is 6.1% better than mcsGARCH at 15 minutes; the full hybrid is 5.8% better.

Correction = hybrid / mcsGARCH, the factor LightGBM multiplied mcsGARCH's forecast by.

How to read QLIKE. QLIKE(y, f) = y / f - log(y / f) - 1 scores a variance forecast f against the realized variance y. It is 0 for a perfect forecast and grows as the forecast misses; forecasting too low is punished more than forecasting too high. Both models were fitted only on days before each test day (walk-forward), so every forecast here was made without seeing the future.
