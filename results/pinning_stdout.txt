                item                                                                                                                                                 change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    reason
    settlement price                                                                    Use NSE's official close as the outcome, not the mean of 15:00 to 15:29 minute bars                                         The bhavcopy settlement price of every expiring option equals the official close exactly on every expiry day (checked here), while the minute-bar mean differs from it by about 1 point (median).
    max-gamma strike                                                                                                           Defined from gross (unsigned) gamma exposure                                                                                         A signed 'largest net GEX' strike depends on the unobserved sign; the sign enters only in the conditioning step, where all three rules are shown.
       movement test                                                                                   Added the signed move toward the strike as the main movement measure The change in absolute distance has a positive mean under a pure random walk (Jensen's inequality) that grows with volatility, and expiry days are more volatile; the signed move has mean zero under no pinning whatever the volatility.
        implied vols                                                                                          Interpolate missing IVs along strikes after sorting by strike                     UDiFF bhavcopy rows are not in strike order; interpolating in file order fills deep options with IVs of unrelated strikes. (data.load_bhavcopy now sorts rows; the script sorts again so it does not depend on that.)
   re-dated expiries                                                     If no contract with expiry E is listed on t-1, use the contract listed within 3 calendar days of E                                                      Holiday changes re-date contracts: on 27 Jun 2023 the Nifty contract was still listed for 29 Jun but expired on 28 Jun; Bank Nifty contracts were re-dated in Sep 2023 and Jan 2025.
     closing auction Split the Tuesday regime at 3 Aug 2026 and restrict settlement-window bar measures (15:29 bar, 15:00 to 15:29 mean, intraday figure) to days before it     NSE's closing auction session went live on 3 Aug 2026 (circular NSE/CMTR/75479); the official close then comes from an auction and the minute index is frozen from 15:15, so the 30-minute bar mean no longer approximates the close.
regime heterogeneity                                                       Added pairwise tests of whether the expiry effect differs between regimes, and a half-year table                                                                           The first run showed opposite signs in the Thursday and Tuesday regimes; whether they really differ is a test, not something to read off two separate p-values.
 gamma near a strike                          Dealer gamma at a strike is evaluated as if the index sat at that strike with 30 trading minutes left (the settlement window)                                          With one full day left, gamma at a level spreads over about +/-5 strikes; with 30 minutes left it weights the strike itself and its immediate neighbours, which is what 'near the strike' means.
