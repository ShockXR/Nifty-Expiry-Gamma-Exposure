# Risk register (Agent 4): niftygex

Summary in plain language: the forecasting headline is numerically right and robust at 15 and 30 minutes. The risks are a look-ahead I found in how the benchmark's daily volatility model is chosen (impact on the headline unknown), a weaker last-hour result than the pitch suggests, the risk of reading "beats mcsGARCH" as "has found information", and a GEX calculation that does no input checking. Likelihood is given only where I measured it.
