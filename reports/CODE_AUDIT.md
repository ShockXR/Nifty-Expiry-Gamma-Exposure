# Code audit: Nifty expiry gamma exposure (niftygex)

**Plain-language summary (for a beginner)**

1. We re-ran the full test suite (708 passed, 7 skipped because the raw market files are not in the repository) and then read the option maths, the forecast ladder and the evaluation code looking for hidden use of future data and wrong units.
2. The option formulas are right: put-call parity, gamma, vega, vanna and charm all match independent finite-difference checks, implied volatility round-trips, and the GEX formula and sign rules behave as documented.
3. The walk-forward forecasts are causal up to a tiny numerical effect (1e-4 relative). The one real look-ahead is a model-selection gate that peeks at later days; the authors already disclosed it, and we reproduced it.
4. Small defects remain in edge cases (last days of a sample get the wrong days-to-expiry, one missing minute bar blanks features for the rest of its day); they are reported, not fixed, because fixing them could change numbers that appear in the documents. One crash (empty input to the Diebold-Mariano test) is fixed.
5. No printed number changed. The number tracer gives 678 claims, 664 matching, 14 untraced (printed integers such as 715 tests), no mismatch.

## Files read closely

`greeks.py`, `gex.py`, `implied.py`, `realized.py`, `volatility.py` (EGARCH wrapper and mcsGARCH fit, filter, forecast), `forecast.py` (walk-forward), `features.py` (alignment of every feature to the forecast time), `evaluation.py`, `inference.py`, `data.py`, `expiry_calendar.py`.

Verified by test (SYNTHETIC data, fixed seeds): Black-76 put-call parity; gamma > 0 and equal to the finite-difference second derivative for calls and puts; delta, vega, vanna, charm against finite differences; implied-vol round trip to 1e-7 over a grid of 5 moneyness, 5 maturities (1 to 63 trading days) and 4 vols; NaN outside no-arbitrage bounds; GEX equals g * OI * lot * S^2 * 0.01 and scales linearly; shares with lot 1 equals contracts with the real lot; sign rules (long_all = minus short_all, US convention nets to zero on a symmetric chain, swapping call and put labels negates it, mirror flips participant weights); gamma flip is a root of net GEX; QLIKE minimised at the sample mean, scale free, asymmetric; Diebold-Mariano sign convention and symmetry; Holm by hand; weekly expiry rule on known dates (Thursday to 28 Aug 2025, Tuesday from 2 Sep 2025, holiday roll-back, regime boundaries 19/20 Nov 2024 and 29 Aug/1 Sep 2025, monthly = last weekly); features for days before a cut and walk-forward forecasts for the first test block do not change when everything after the cut is altered (day-level inputs and the naive forecast bit-exact, fitted models within 1e-4 relative); walk-forward and simulator replay bit-identical for a fixed seed.
